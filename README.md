# The World Today — 21 November 2026 in one image

Entry for make.fun `news-image-20261121` — sum up 21 November 2026's world
news in one image. $100 pot, 50 paid places (1st $20, 2nd $12, 3rd $6,
4th–10th $2, 11th–50th $1.20). Deadline: 2026-11-23T00:00:00Z
(2026-11-23 08:00 Asia/Shanghai).

## How it works

`main.py` (stdlib only, no network, no clock) copies the bundled
`news.png` to `/output/entry.png`. The image is 1024×1024, ~117 KB,
well under the 1 MiB limit. Two runs write identical bytes.

`news.png` was generated locally by `generate.py` (Pillow, fixed text /
colours / layout) as a front-page poster:

- **Lead:** Russian Kh-101 missiles and a drone crash in Poland —
  Tusk calls it an intentional provocation, NATO's Rutte a grave
  violation; no injuries reported.
- Ukraine under fire: 400+ drones, 110+ missiles overnight, 8+ killed.
- COP31 in Antalya stalls / into overtime; draft drops fossil fuels.
- Wall Street AI rout: Nasdaq erases 2026 gains, S&P 500 into correction.
- Denmark expels Russia's envoy; Saudi forces intercept a Houthi
  missile, debris in Riyadh.

Stories verified from AP / Reuters reporting on 21 Nov 2026.
